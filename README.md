# NexaMart — AI Customer Support Agent Deployment

## Self-Directed Business Analysis Case Study | 2026

### Overview

A self-directed enterprise Business Analysis case study exploring
the discovery, analysis and design of an AI-powered customer
support transformation.

NexaMart is a fictional multinational e-commerce organization
created for portfolio and professional learning purposes.

---

## Business Problem

NexaMart is exploring AI-enabled customer support to address
repetitive customer inquiries, manual information retrieval,
response-time pressure and opportunities for 24/7 support.

---

## Business Objective

The objective is to assess the business, operational and
technology considerations involved in introducing an AI-powered
customer support agent while maintaining appropriate human
escalation, governance and security controls.
