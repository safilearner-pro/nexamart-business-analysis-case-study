## NexaMart AI Customer Support Agent Deployment

### Objective

To assess the internal organizational capabilities and external
environmental factors relevant to NexaMart's proposed AI-powered
customer support initiative.

### Technique

SWOT Analysis

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

### Analysis Outcome

The analysis identified potential organizational strengths and
market opportunities, while highlighting knowledge quality,
legacy integrations, AI governance, data security and human
escalation as areas requiring further investigation.

### Deliverables

- SWOT analysis report
- SWOT working spreadsheet
- SWOT matrix visualization
- Assumptions and evidence assessment
- Strategic implications
- Recommendations

### Case Study Status

Completed — September 2026

### Classification

Self-directed simulated enterprise case study.

or employer engagement.
